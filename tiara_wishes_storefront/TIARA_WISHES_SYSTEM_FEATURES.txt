TIARA WISHES
Functionality and features
Written from the storefront, admin app, and Flask API as they stand.

The system is a household and lifestyle shop. Shoppers buy with cash. Digital Chamas are described in the shop but cannot take a payment, hold a wallet, or track contributions.


1. THE THREE PARTS

Storefront
  Customer shop. React and Vite. Local address http://localhost:5174
  Reads the catalogue, prices, contact details, and homepage copy from the API.
  Does not include staff tools.

Admin
  Staff workspace. React and Vite. Local address http://localhost:5173/admin/login
  Publishes products, stock, orders, payments, deliveries, promotions, and site content.
  Each screen is shown only when the signed-in staff member has the matching permission.

API
  Flask service. Local address http://127.0.0.1:5055
  Prefix /api/v1
  Stores data in MySQL. Serves uploaded images from /media
  The storefront dev server proxies /api and /media to the API.


2. CUSTOMER SHOP

Home
  Announcement bar from published site content, with a fallback line when none is set.
  Hero for new arrivals, with the housewares photo on the right.
  A save note appears only when a featured product has a higher compare-at price.
  Category cards in one horizontal row, with arrows. Each card uses an icon for that department.
  Featured products in rows of up to eight, scrolled with arrows.
  Empty space can show a store promotion marked for that use, otherwise featured products, otherwise a prompt to request an unlisted item.
  Chama teaser, marked coming soon. It links to an explanation page and is not a way to pay.

Browse and search
  Shop all, and a department strip built from published categories.
  Search from the header. A search with no matches offers a request for that product.
  Filters for category, brand, and price. Sort by newest, price, or name.
  Product rows scroll sideways per row. The whole list scrolls downward. Scrollbars are hidden and arrows are used.
  On a small screen the filter sits above the products.

Product page
  One card with the photo, brand, name, cash price, compare-at price when it is higher, stock, short description, quantity, and Add to cart.
  Further details in the same card when the admin entered them: key features, specifications, what is in the box, and the longer description.
  Specifications can include SKU, model, size, dimensions, weight, material, warranty, brand, and extra label-and-value rows.
  Watch video appears only when the admin saved a usage video link. YouTube, Vimeo, a full TikTok video link, Instagram, and a direct video file play in the page. A short share link opens in a new tab.
  Out of stock products can be requested from the shop.
  Approved reviews are listed when the product has any. The shop does not invent ratings.
  More products from the same category.

Cart
  Guests can add items before signing in. The cart is kept for the browser session and mirrored for the guest.
  Change quantity or remove a line. Unavailable lines are marked.
  The top bar uses a cart icon and the item count.
  Signing in can merge the guest cart into the account cart.

Checkout
  Sign in or register is required before the order is placed.
  Delivery address, chosen from saved addresses or entered for Kenya.
  Optional order note and promotion code. The quote is recalculated from current prices.
  Pay now is the working choice. Pay with chama is shown as coming soon and cannot be selected.
  Payment uses the configured provider. M-Pesa asks for the phone that should receive the prompt. A mock provider exists for non-production testing.
  The shop does not mark an order paid from the browser. Payment is confirmed by the API.
  Placing an order uses an idempotency key so a repeat submit does not create a second order.

Account
  Profile: first name, last name, and phone.
  Change password.
  Saved addresses: add, edit, delete, and a default address.
  Orders list and an order detail page.
  Recent notifications, which can be marked read.
  Sign out.

Sign-in
  Register, sign in, forgot password, and reset password.
  The access token stays in the browser tab. The refresh token stays in an HttpOnly cookie.

Contact and policies
  Product request form: name, phone, email, product name, description, quantity, optional budget, and a photo.
  Sending opens an email to the shop address published in settings. It does not place an order, and the photo is not uploaded to the shop.
  Published email, phone, and WhatsApp.
  A map of Kenya. A street pin is not shown until the store publishes an address.
  About, delivery and returns, privacy, and terms use published content blocks, with plain placeholders when a block is missing.

Chamas
  Informational only. A shopper cannot join, contribute, or pay through a Chama.
  Rewards in the header opens that same explanation. It is not a points balance.


3. STAFF ADMIN

Access
  Staff sign in at /admin/login.
  Roles that can be assigned: customer, admin, inventory manager, order manager, finance manager, content manager, and super admin.
  Screens and API actions check permissions such as products.manage, inventory.manage, orders.manage, customers.read, customers.manage, payments.manage, deliveries.manage, promotions.manage, reviews.moderate, content.manage, users.manage, reports.read, and audit.read.
  The sidebar hides items the person cannot use, and shows counts for the sections they can open.
  Chama in the admin menu is marked for later and does not operate.

Dashboard
  Paid sales and paid orders for a chosen period.
  Pending orders and pending payments.
  Products at or below their low-stock threshold.

Products
  Create, edit, and archive products.
  Fields include name, slug, SKU, category, brand, short description, description, cash price, compare-at price, currency, featured flag, active flag, and status (draft, active, archived).
  Usage video link.
  Handling: normal, delicate, or perishable.
  Model, material, size, warranty, key features, box contents, and specification rows.
  Weight and dimensions.
  Opening stock on create, and a low-stock threshold.
  Upload images, delete an image, and reorder images. One image is primary.
  Search and meta title and description for the product.

Categories and brands
  Create, edit, and delete categories, including a parent so departments can be nested.
  Create, edit, and delete brands, including a logo and description.

Inventory
  On-hand quantity, reserved quantity, and available quantity.
  Adjustments: restock, return, opening stock, and correction.
  Each adjustment is recorded.

Orders
  List and open an order.
  Move an order only along the allowed path:
    pending payment can become cancelled or failed
    confirmed can become processing or refund pending
    processing can become ready to dispatch or refund pending
    ready to dispatch can become dispatched or refund pending
    dispatched can become delivered
    refund pending can become refunded
    failed can become cancelled
    delivered, cancelled, and refunded do not move further
  Payment status on an order can be unpaid, pending, paid, failed, partially refunded, or refunded.

Customers
  List customers and open a customer.
  Activate or deactivate an account when the staff member can manage customers.
  Assign roles when the staff member can manage users.

Payments
  List payments and open one by its merchant reference.
  Statuses: initiated, pending, succeeded, failed, cancelled, refunded, partially refunded.
  Record a refund. Recording a refund restates the original payment in reports. It does not send money back through the payment provider by itself.
  Providers implemented in the API: mock (disabled in production) and M-Pesa, including a webhook for the provider to confirm payment.

Deliveries
  List deliveries and update a delivery.
  Statuses: pending, processing, packed, dispatched, in transit, delivered, failed, returned.
  Delivery places with their own fees. Shoppers can load the published places. Staff can create, edit, and delete places.
  Store settings also hold a delivery fee and a free-delivery threshold in Kenya shillings.

Promotions
  Percentage or fixed amount.
  Optional code, start, end, minimum order amount, and usage limit.
  Active flag.
  A flag to show the promotion in empty space on the shop.
  The code is checked when the shopper requests a checkout quote.

Reviews
  Shoppers can submit a review on a product.
  Staff moderate reviews. Only approved reviews are counted and shown on the product page.

Content
  Announcements, advertisements, and video links for the storefront.
  A video entry requires an https link.
  Homepage blocks, including the announcement bar, about copy, and the Chama teaser, come from this content.

Settings
  Contact email, phone, and WhatsApp.
  Social links for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.
  Currency.
  Delivery fee and free-delivery threshold.

Staff
  See staff accounts and assign the roles listed above.

Reports
  Sales report: paid sales and paid order count between two dates.
  Paid sales count succeeded payments, and partially refunded payments after recorded refunds. Pending, failed, cancelled, and fully refunded payments are left out.
  Product report: units and revenue by product for paid orders in the period.

Audit logs
  Staff actions that change the shop are written to an audit log and can be reviewed by someone with audit access.


4. HOW THE PIECES FIT

  The admin publishes a product, its price, images, stock, and optional video.
  The storefront lists only active products and reads those fields from the API.
  A guest cart is stored against a cart session. After sign-in it can be merged.
  Checkout quotes the current prices and any valid promotion, then creates an order.
  Payment is started with the configured provider. The order stays unpaid until the API records a successful payment.
  Staff then move the order and its delivery through the status paths above.
  Stock available to shoppers is on-hand quantity minus reserved quantity.


5. NOT IN THIS RELEASE

  Chama joining, contributions, schedules, member position, or payment.
  A rewards or points wallet. The Rewards control only opens the Chama explanation.
  A wishlist.
  A stored inbox for product requests. The request opens the shopper's email app.
  A published street address or a map pin. The contact map is Kenya until an address is published.
  Marking a payment as paid from the shopper's browser.
  Sending a refund to M-Pesa or another provider automatically. Staff record the refund in the system.
